# NexusCloud Product Overview

## What is NexusCloud?

NexusCloud is a comprehensive cloud computing platform designed for businesses of all sizes. We provide scalable compute, storage, and content delivery services with enterprise-grade security and reliability.

## Core Services

### NexusCloud Compute
Deploy virtual machines running Linux or Windows with flexible configurations. Features include auto-scaling, load balancing, and support for containerized workloads. Available in four global regions.

### NexusCloud Storage
Secure object and block storage with built-in CDN integration. All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption. Supports file versioning and lifecycle management policies.

### NexusCloud CDN
Global content delivery network with edge nodes in over 50 locations worldwide. Reduces latency for end users by caching static assets close to their location.

## Pricing Tiers

NexusCloud offers three pricing tiers to match your needs:

- **Free Tier**: Ideal for development and testing. Includes 5GB storage, 1 vCPU, and basic community support. No credit card required to start.
- **Pro Tier ($29.99/month)**: Built for growing businesses. Includes 100GB storage, 4 vCPU, priority email support, and live chat during business hours.
- **Enterprise Tier ($99.99/month)**: For mission-critical workloads. Includes 500GB storage, 16 vCPU, a dedicated account manager, 24/7 phone support, and a 99.99% uptime SLA.

## Getting Started

Sign up at nexuscloud.io to create your account. Free tier accounts are provisioned instantly. Pro and Enterprise plans can be activated from your dashboard under Settings > Billing. All plans include access to the NexusCloud dashboard, API, and CLI tools.

## Platform Highlights

NexusCloud is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit with TLS 1.3 and at rest with AES-256. Enterprise customers can opt for dedicated tenancy for full resource isolation.
