# NexusCloud Billing FAQ

## Payment Methods

NexusCloud accepts the following payment methods:
- **Credit and debit cards**: Visa, Mastercard, American Express
- **Wire transfer**: Available for Enterprise tier customers only, with net-30 payment terms
- **PayPal**: Available for Pro and Enterprise tiers

## Billing Cycle

Your billing cycle starts on the day you sign up or upgrade your plan. Monthly charges are processed on the same date each month. If your billing date falls on a day that doesn't exist in a given month (e.g., the 31st), you will be billed on the last day of that month.

## Viewing Invoices

To access your invoices:
1. Log in to the NexusCloud dashboard
2. Navigate to Settings > Billing > Invoices
3. Click on any invoice to view details or download a PDF copy

Invoices are generated within 24 hours of each billing cycle and are retained for 12 months.

## Auto-Renewal

All NexusCloud subscriptions auto-renew at the end of each billing period. You can disable auto-renewal from Settings > Billing > Subscription. If auto-renewal is disabled, your plan will revert to the Free tier at the end of the current period.

## Updating Payment Information

To update your payment method:
1. Go to Settings > Billing > Payment Methods
2. Click "Add New Payment Method"
3. Enter your new card details
4. Set it as the default payment method

## Failed Payments

If a payment fails, NexusCloud will retry the charge up to 3 times over 7 days. You will receive email notifications for each retry. If all retries fail, your account will be downgraded to the Free tier, but your data will be preserved for 30 days.

## Tax Information

Prices listed do not include applicable taxes. Sales tax or VAT will be applied based on your billing address. Enterprise customers can provide a tax-exempt certificate by contacting billing@nexuscloud.io.
