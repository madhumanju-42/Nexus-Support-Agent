# NexusCloud Refund Policy

## Annual Plans

Annual plan subscribers are eligible for a full refund within the first 30 days of purchase. After 30 days, no refunds are available for annual plans. To request a refund, submit a support ticket through the dashboard or email support@nexuscloud.io.

## Monthly Plans

Monthly plan subscribers can cancel at any time. If you cancel within the first 14 days of a billing cycle, you will receive a pro-rated refund for the remaining days. Cancellations after 14 days will not receive a refund, but you will retain access until the end of the current billing period.

## Overage Charges

Overage charges for storage or bandwidth usage beyond your plan's included limits are non-refundable. These charges reflect actual resource consumption and are billed at the rates specified in your plan.

## Refund Process

1. Submit a refund request via the dashboard (Settings > Billing > Request Refund) or email support@nexuscloud.io
2. Include your account email and the reason for the refund
3. Our billing team will review your request within 2 business days
4. Approved refunds are processed within 5-7 business days
5. Refunds are returned to the original payment method

## Credits

In lieu of a refund, you may request account credits that can be applied to future billing cycles. Credits do not expire and can be used for any NexusCloud service.

## Disputes

If you believe you were charged in error, contact billing@nexuscloud.io with your invoice number and a description of the issue. We will investigate and respond within 3 business days.
