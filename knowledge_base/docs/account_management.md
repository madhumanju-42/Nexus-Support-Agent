# NexusCloud Account Management

## Password Reset

If you've forgotten your password:
1. Click "Forgot Password" on the login page
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Check your inbox for a password reset link (valid for 24 hours)
4. Click the link and set a new password

Password requirements: minimum 12 characters, at least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number, and one special character.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

We strongly recommend enabling 2FA for added security:
1. Go to Settings > Security > Two-Factor Authentication
2. Choose your method: Authenticator app (recommended) or SMS
3. For authenticator apps, scan the QR code with Google Authenticator, Authy, or any TOTP-compatible app
4. Enter the verification code to confirm setup

If you lose access to your 2FA device, contact support with your account recovery code (provided during 2FA setup).

## Team Member Management

Administrators can manage team members from Settings > Team:
- **Admin role**: Full access including billing, settings, and team management
- **Editor role**: Can create, modify, and delete resources but cannot access billing or manage team members
- **Viewer role**: Read-only access to resources and dashboards

To invite a team member, click "Invite Member," enter their email, and select their role. Invitations expire after 7 days.

## Account Deletion

To delete your NexusCloud account:
1. Go to Settings > Account > Delete Account
2. Confirm by entering your password
3. Your account enters a 30-day grace period during which you can reactivate by logging in
4. After 30 days, all data is permanently deleted and cannot be recovered

Before deleting, download any data you wish to keep. Active subscriptions will be cancelled and refunded according to our refund policy.
