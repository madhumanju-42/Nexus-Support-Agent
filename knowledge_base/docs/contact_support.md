# Contacting NexusCloud Support

## Support Channels by Plan

### Free Tier
- **Community Forum**: Access the NexusCloud community at community.nexuscloud.io to ask questions, share tips, and connect with other users.
- **Email Support**: Send inquiries to support@nexuscloud.io. Expected response time is 48 hours during business days.

### Pro Tier
- **Email Support**: Priority email support with a 24-hour response time during business days.
- **Live Chat**: Available during business hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET. Access live chat from the dashboard by clicking the chat icon in the bottom-right corner.

### Enterprise Tier
- **24/7 Phone Support**: Call 1-800-NEXUS-00 (1-800-639-8700) any time. Enterprise support engineers are available around the clock.
- **Dedicated Account Manager**: Your account manager is your primary point of contact for strategic guidance, onboarding, and escalations.
- **Priority Email and Chat**: Enterprise tickets are prioritized with a 4-hour response time.

## Submitting a Support Ticket

For all tiers, you can submit a support ticket through the dashboard:
1. Click the "Help" button in the top navigation bar
2. Select "Submit a Ticket"
3. Choose a category: Billing, Technical, Account, or General
4. Describe your issue in detail
5. Attach screenshots or logs if applicable
6. Submit — you will receive a confirmation email with your ticket number

## Escalation Process

If your issue is not resolved to your satisfaction:
1. Reply to your support ticket requesting escalation
2. Email escalation@nexuscloud.io with your ticket number
3. For Enterprise customers, contact your dedicated account manager directly
4. For urgent production issues, Enterprise customers can call 1-800-NEXUS-00 and request immediate escalation

## Support Hours

- **Email**: Business hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET (all tiers)
- **Live Chat**: Business hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET (Pro and Enterprise)
- **Phone**: 24/7 (Enterprise only)

## Feedback

We value your feedback. After each support interaction, you will receive a satisfaction survey. You can also send feedback directly to feedback@nexuscloud.io.
